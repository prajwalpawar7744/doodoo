Scope: home screen (`/`), mode: experience. Friends on a call open the link and hop into a room.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The home screen is a two-ink risograph print, not an app screen. It refuses the centred-logo-over-a-rounded-button party-game default.
OWN-WORLD: White uncoated paper with real grain. Two inks only: riso violet (deep lilac, carries text) and riso yellow. Where they overlap they multiply into a muddy overprint. Layers sit a few px off-register. Chunky display lettering; doodled arrows and squiggles printed in yellow.
STORY: Visitors see a friendly handmade print, read "doodoo" and know it's a drawing game, then press the one stamped button to join.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Giant two-layer "doodoo" logo centred slightly above middle, filling most of the width. The yellow layer is offset under the violet one. Below, the stamped "Hop in & doodle!" button with a hand-drawn yellow arrow curling toward it. A few yellow squiggles near the edges.
FORM: Riso zine print, grounded candidate 7 of 7, raised by the hand-drawn zine: doodle arrows point at the action. seed:5497d40910bb1f11
SIGNATURE: On load the logo layers slide into near-register. Pressing the button pulls its two ink layers into perfect register like a stamp landing. Reduced motion shows the resting state.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
