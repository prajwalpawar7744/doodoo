# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
Small groups of friends (about 3–8) already together on a voice/video call (Discord, Zoom), who want a quick game to play together. They join a private room through an invite link or room code.

## Product Purpose
doodoo is a multiplayer drawing-and-guessing game: one player draws a secret word, the others race to guess it in chat. Success is friends laughing at each other's drawings and wanting another round.

## Positioning
Cozy and cute rather than competitive: soft, warm, a little silly, feeling hand-made.

## Operating Context
Played alongside a call, so talking happens outside the app. Desktop first; phones are a nice-to-have.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Private rooms by code/link; host starts the game and sets rounds and draw time.
- Server owns the word, timer and scoring; the client renders state it receives.
- Backend is built separately; the event contract lives in `plan.md`.
- Open: scoring formula, whether the drawer picks from 3 words, whether public rooms come later.

## Brand Commitments
- Name: "doodoo", written lowercase.
- Colour direction: lilac (set by the user).

## Evidence on Hand
No logo, illustrations, testimonials or player counts exist. Don't invent any.

## Product Principles
1. Get friends into a room in seconds.
2. Cozy over competitive: losing should still be fun.
3. The drawings are the show; the interface stays out of their way.
